# AI Collaboration Log: Final Project Proposal

This log is my attribution for the GenAI help I used on this proposal.

**Tool:** Claude (Anthropic), through Claude Code, November 3 and November 16, 2026.

## How we worked

I chose the data and the questions, and Claude did the checking and the building. Claude queried
the live archive for counts and coverage, proposed the statistics, drafted the proposal text from
our discussion, and wrote the two scripts and the chart. I reviewed each part, asked questions
until the methods made sense to me, and made the final calls.

## What came from me

- NASA exoplanet data as the dataset.
- The habitable zone as the project's one hypothesis. I cut the original hypothesis about
  orbital distance so the proposal focuses on it. Also the question of whether the planets in
  the zone are gas or rock.
- The predictions in stage 4. My first one was that transit would find the largest share of zone
  planets. Claude showed me the typical transit planet orbits at 0.08 AU, far inside the zone of
  a Sun-like star, and I revised it. The point that a fixed distance like 1 AU only marks the
  zone for stars like the Sun is mine.
- The decision to go past a single test and add the later stages.

## What came from Claude

- Using orbital distance instead of orbital period, which is missing for most microlensing and
  imaging planets.
- The choice of tests, the classifier in stage 3 and its prediction, which I agreed with.
- The limits listed under the models.

## How it was checked

Every count comes from the committed snapshot. The habitable zone limits and the rocky size
cutoff were read from the two cited papers. No test was run before the hypotheses were written.
